### 台灣牌規則
- Definition：The Taiwanese Mahjong scoring rule set implemented by this calculator, defining all hand types (牌型), their fan values (番數), and exclusion rules that prevent duplicate counting of overlapping hand types.
- Aliases：Taiwanese mahjong rules、TW rules

### 門清
- Definition：Closed-hand status: the player has not declared any pung/kong from other players' discards. Affects eligibility for several hand types and bonus fans such as 門清自摸 and 門清大叮.
- Aliases：closed hand、menqin

### 宣告聽牌（叮）
- Definition：Declaring readiness to win (ting/tenpai) at a specific moment; once declared, the hand cannot be changed and certain actions like open kong are restricted. Grants 5 fan and enables related bonuses.
- Aliases：ding、declare ting

### 大雞糊 / 鴨糊
- Definition：Special high-scoring hands: 大雞糊 scores 30 base plus 5底 plus reward-type fans when only one non-reward fan is present; 鴨糊 is the self-drawn variant scoring 10+5+1 plus rewards. Both depend on accurate classification of 'non-reward' fans.
- Aliases：big chicken、duck hu

### 花糊
- Definition：Immediate-win hand types triggered by flower tiles: 兩台花 (80 fan), 七搶一 (20 fan), and 一搶七 (20 fan). These settle immediately upon drawing/claiming flowers without evaluating the rest of the hand.
- Aliases：flower hu

### 嚦咕嚦咕
- Definition：Eight-pair hand (all pairs, no melds); counts as a special category with its own fan value and can stack with additional hand types such as 缺一門, 混一色, or 混帶X.
- Aliases：eight pairs

### 四歸類
- Definition：Hand type category where all four copies of one tile are used within the hand (四歸一, 四歸二, 四歸四), granting 5–40 fan depending on how the fourth copy is employed (eye vs sequence).
- Aliases：four-of-a-kind

### 氣通貫類
- Definition：Category of complete 1-9 sequences in a single suit: 明龍 (open, 10 fan) and 暗龍 (self-drawn last tile, 20 fan), plus mixed-suit variants 明雜龍 (5) and 暗雜龍 (10).
- Aliases：full sequence

### 同門類
- Definition：Hand types requiring coverage across suits and honor categories: 五門齊 (10/15 fan) and 七門齊 (20/25 fan), where wind and dragon honors each count as one 'door'; also includes 缺一門, 混一色, 清一色.
- Aliases：multi-suit

### 特珠類
- Definition：Special standalone hand types including 十三么 (140/150 fan), 十六不搭 (60/70 fan), 嚦咕嚦咕六/八飛 (60 fan), and various multi-winner bonuses (雪上霜, 雪上冰, 錦上添花).
- Aliases：special hands
